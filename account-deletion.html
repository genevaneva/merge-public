Request Account Deletion

App: Amatory

Last Updated: April 30, 2026

You can delete your Amatory account and associated data from inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email.

Request by Email

Send an email to support with the address connected to your Amatory account. Include "Delete my Amatory account" in the subject line.

Email Deletion Request

If the button does not open your email app, send the request manually to support@loreedit.app.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and authentication profile
  • Your profile information
  • Relationship and partner-linking data associated with your account
  • Encounters, notes, ratings, tags, and timeline entries associated with your account
  • Photos and uploaded media associated with your account

Retention

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before permanent removal.

Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

Verification

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deleting it. This protects your account from unauthorized deletion requests.
